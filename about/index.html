About the IWC Lab

The IWC Lab is a sandbox next to the Intergalactic Workflow Commission. Workflows here are experimental: they are tested and deployed the same way IWC's are, but they have not been through IWC review.

What's here

  • Workflows written in Galaxy's Format-2
  • Workflows being tried out before they are proposed to IWC

Where they come from

Everything on this site is built from galaxyproject/iwc-lab. Workflows merged there are published to the iwc-lab-workflows GitHub organization and registered on Dockstore, which is where "Run in Galaxy" loads them from. Nothing here is installed on the usegalaxy.* servers.

For reviewed, production-ready workflows, use the IWC workflow library.
